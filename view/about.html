About Jeopardy

What is Jeopardy

Jeopardy, is a famous American TV quiz show with a fun twist: the clues are given as answers, and players have to respond with a question. It was created by Merv Griffin and first aired in 1964 with Art Fleming as the host. In 1984, the show came back with Alex Trebek, who hosted it for 36 years. Today, it’s one of the most popular game shows in the world, known for its tough trivia and for Ken Jennings’ record 74-game winning streak.

How to play?

  • Start the game: Click “Start Game” on the home page. The board loads with a set of categories, each with clues worth different points.
  • Pick a clue: Click any tile on the board. Tiles with higher points usually have harder clues.
  • Read the clue: The clue appears on the screen.
  • Think of your answer: On the show, answers are phrased as questions, like “What is Paris?” Here, you can keep it simple and just focus on knowing the answer.
  • Reveal the answer: Click to see if you were right.
  • Move on: Once a tile is used, it’s marked so you can’t pick it again.
  • Finish the board: When every tile has been used, the game is over.
